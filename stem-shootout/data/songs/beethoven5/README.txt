Qualitative demo only - NOT a scored catalog entry.

Beethoven's Symphony No. 5, 1st movement, public domain (Musopen recording,
via archive.org). No isolated per-instrument ground truth exists for this
specific recording, so it cannot be scored with museval the way every other
song in this catalog is - this project's own rule is "measured, not judged
by ear," and there's nothing to measure against here. Used once to
characterize htdemucs_6s's behavior on real orchestral content by energy
distribution across its 6 stems (see HANDOFF.md, "Cloud research task
continued" section, 2026-10-06) - not to be mistaken for a reference_raw/
ground-truth folder like the other songs in data/songs/ have.

beethoven5_mvt1.mp3 is gitignored, same as every other song's audio.
